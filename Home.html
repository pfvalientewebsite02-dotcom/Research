Welcome

Welcome to the official website of the Work Immersion Program of Tayabas Western Academy. This platform provides Grade 12 students with important information, updates, and guidelines related to their work immersion.

Objectives

  • Expose students to real workplace environments
  • Devolop skills, discipline, and work ethics
  • Prepare students for employment or higher education
  • Enhance responsibility and professionalism

Who can Join?

This program is intended for Grade 12 students of Tayabas Western Academy who are currently enrolled in Senior High School.
